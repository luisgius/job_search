## Peer challenge and proposed order

The strategy peer proposed recovering useful known employers and building an open-opportunity explorer plus loss funnel before paying for broader discovery. **Agree on measurement and rejecting thousands of speculative slugs; challenge making the explorer a prerequisite for every retrieval repair.** Allegro's official full page is readable now, exhibits an unsupported SAP surface, and contains a near-level commercial-ML role. A narrowly scoped Allegro public-site collector can be validated alongside the explorer, whereas a generic Workday effort currently rests here on an unreadable Motorola detail and no measured incremental suitable yield.

Proposed order, as a research recommendation rather than authorization to edit:

1. Capture a small Allegro public listing/detail fixture set; verify canonical IDs, dates/unknown dates, full descriptions, Poland locations and closure behavior. Use the existing near-level role as a coverage check, not as proof of eligibility. Do not use tenant-admin OData or invent dates to pass the 72-hour gate.
2. Add the 12 employers to a **reviewable discovery registry artifact** with source URL, last check, platform evidence, check outcome and next action; implementation/configuration remains a subsequent task. Keep HERE and Hitachi closed requisitions as negative controls.
3. Run the same bounded comparison for Motorola/Workday and an AstraZeneca public career surface, then select the reusable adapter by additional unique, readable, apparently open Poland roles—not employer count or total board size. AstraZeneca's clinical/R focus may yield little for this particular profile despite a working page.
4. Sample ASPIRE and Pracuj for new employer names only after the registry can distinguish stale leads, migrations and failures. Compare incremental useful roles per request and engineering effort against the known-employer repair, retaining the loss funnel and exploratory view throughout.

**Question for peer review:** should the first implementation deliver a single verified Allegro route plus measurements, or a Workday adapter whose broader theoretical coverage has not yet produced a readable, candidate-relevant detail in this pilot? The evidence favors Allegro first; a small live Workday sample showing better net suitable yield would change that ordering.
